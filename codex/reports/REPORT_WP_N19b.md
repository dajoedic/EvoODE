## Selection

The harness now accepts three optional config fields: `dimensions`, `system_ids`, and `max_systems`. The filters are combined in deterministic `system_id` order. - `test_smoke_writes_sindy_and_odeformer_records`: runs the smoke config on real exported trajectories and checks generated `records.csv`, `trajectory_check.csv`, and `selected_systems.json`.

- `test_selection_combines_dimension_id_and_limit_filters`: uses `benchmarks/data/strogatz_extended.json`; expected IDs come from applying the requested filters in deterministic `system_id` order.
